    How space weather affects Earth and space missions

    Space weather is driven by the Sun's flares, coronal mass ejections and solar wind. When this energy reaches Earth it disturbs our magnetic field and upper atmosphere.

    🛰️ SatellitesCharged particles can damage electronics, and a heated atmosphere increases drag on low-orbit satellites.
    📡 CommunicationFlares disturb the ionosphere and can black out HF radio, especially on the sunlit side.
    🧭 NavigationIonospheric disturbances reduce GPS accuracy by delaying and scrambling signals.
    👩‍🚀 AstronautsSolar radiation storms raise radiation exposure, so crews may shelter in shielded areas.
    ⚡ Power gridsStrong geomagnetic storms induce currents that can overload transformers.
    🌌 AuroraThe visible upside: storms push auroras to lower latitudes.
